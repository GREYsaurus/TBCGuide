# Roaming hunting routes

On a kill step with a verified route, the waypoint arrow walks a loop of points through the area where the target mobs are known to spawn. When there's no route, it points at one spot. This is navigation only. TBCGuide never moves the character, targets mobs, starts combat or completes a step because you reached a point.

Setting: **Roaming Waypoints for Kill Objectives** (Settings → General → Features, on by default). Buttons: *Next patrol point* and *Normal objective waypoint*. Commands: `/tbcg route next | off | on`, plus the key binding *Next hunting route point*.

## Coverage

**Definitions:**
- **Unique route:** a distinct set of points. A quest that hunts the same NPCs in the same area as another quest reuses that route's points; it's counted once.
- **Objective supported:** a quest objective (quest + quest-log objective index) that has a route, its own or a reused one.
- **Candidate objective:** an objective from a guide kill or item step that `docs/generated/roaming-candidates.csv` classes as a possible kill or kill-for-drop area. A quest with three kill targets is three candidates.
- **Reviewed:** has any disposition other than *Not researched* in `docs/research/roaming-dispositions.csv`.

- **By faction:** objectives supported, by the first guide that uses them: Alliance 103, Horde 82, both-faction guides 8. A quest used by several guides counts once.
- **Static waypoint preferred:** the spawn area is too small for a loop (fewer than 8 spawns or under 70 yards across), or it's one fixed spawn. A single arrow is the better guide there.
- **Blocked:** what would be needed is missing:
  - **No loop fits:** no non-crossing loop keeps every leg near the spawns (118 objectives: 82 in normal zones, 36 in strict zones). The spawns are scattered or in strips; often a static waypoint is better anyway.
  - **No drop source here:** the dropping NPCs don't spawn in the guide's zone (54).
  - **Underground or multi-level:** the objective is in a cave, mine, indoors or on several levels (45 by text, plus 12 by review).
  - **Too few spawn records:** 3 to 5 (19).
  - **Guide and data disagree:** the guide text names other sources than the quest data (3).
  - **Void edge:** Void Ridge (1).
  - **No target spawns** in the guide's zone (1).
- **Excluded:** elite or rare targets, or drops only from elites.

The per-objective record, with targets, zone, route name, coverage, path check, limits and validation, is `docs/research/roaming-dispositions.csv`. The human decisions behind it are `docs/research/roaming-overrides.csv`. `python -I tools/research/roam_coverage.py` prints the counts above.

## How it works (`TBCGuide/Core/Roam.lua`)

- **When it runs:** the current step is a complete step whose quest has a route, and the quest is in your log. Every objective the route serves must be in the log with the expected type (`monster` for kills, `item` for drops). If the log data is missing or doesn't match, there's no route and you get the normal waypoint.
- **Objectives, not quests:** a route runs while one of its objectives is unfinished. It ends as soon as they're done, whether from your own kills or party credit, even if the quest has other objectives left.
- **Several targets:** with several routes for one quest, the active route stays until its objectives are done; kills of other targets don't switch it. Then the next route in data order starts. The label shows only the unfinished objective, using the quest log's own text (in your client's language), for example "Young Thistle Boar slain: 2/4 - point 3 of 4".
- **Starting point:** the nearest point by distance. If you're already inside it, it starts at the next one. The nearest point by straight-line distance isn't always the shortest or safest walk.
- **Arrival:** distance is measured in yards from `C_Map` world positions, not raw map percentages. A point counts as reached only after you've been seen more than 1.3 × its radius away and then inside its radius. The compiler keeps points at least 2 × radius + 10 yards apart. So standing still, fighting or looting never advances the arrow, and no timer advances it either.
- **Looping:** after the last point the arrow goes back to the first, until the objective is done.
- **Pausing:** with no player position the arrow holds its point, and the step window says "paused". Inside an instance the route stops.
- **Stopping:** the route stops when:
  - the setting is turned off;
  - the step or guide changes;
  - the quest is completed or abandoned;
  - you choose *Normal objective waypoint*.

  It then hands the arrow back to the normal step waypoint.
- **Who controls the arrow** (`Nav.owner`): `step`, `route` or `manual`. A waypoint you asked for, such as `/tbcg train waypoint`, is never replaced by a route or by the periodic objective retargeting. `/tbcg route on` takes the arrow back.
- **Reloads:** nothing is saved. After a reload or login, the route is rebuilt from the quest log, the current step and the nearest point.
- **Cost:** a 0.5-second position check runs only while a route is active. Quest updates come from the quest tracker's events. Route data is static and loaded once.
- **Map:** route points aren't drawn on the world map; the existing map pin shows the current point.

## Route data (`guides-src/routes/*.routes`)

```
zone Elwynn Forest 3470.8x2314.6     # map size in yards, from UiMapAssignment for the zone's uiMap
route Kobold Camp Cleanup: Kobold Vermin
quest 7
kill 1 6                             # objective index in the quest log, NPC id (must be the quest's kill target)
radius 15                            # arrival radius in yards (10-40); a point may set r=NN
loop                                 # required: only looping routes are supported
point 47.6,35.8 | West end of the kobold camp      # optional note after " | "
...
end
```

- **Drop objectives** use `drop <objective> <itemId> <npc,...>`. Each NPC must drop the item according to the item's NPC-drop data.
- **Several objectives:** several `kill`/`drop` lines make one route serve several objectives. Several routes for the same objective are alternatives; the first one in file order with an unfinished objective runs.
- **Output:** the compiler writes `TBCGuide/Guides/Compiled/Routes.lua` (`ns.RoamRoutes`, keyed by quest). Guide steps are unchanged, so guide ids and saved progress are unaffected.

**Compiler checks** (`tools/src/RoamRoutes.cs`; the build fails on any of them):
- the quest exists, and the objective index and kill target match the quest data;
- drop NPCs really drop the item;
- quests that mix kill and item objectives are rejected, because their quest log order can't be checked;
- 3-12 points, a loop, and a radius of 10-40 yards;
- each point within 60 yards of a bundled spawn of a target in the route's zone, and each leg's midpoint within 90 yards;
- every pair of points at least the two radii + 10 yards apart.

The bundled data keeps at most 10 spawns per NPC, so these checks are coarse. The research step below uses the full list.

## Research and verification

**Sources (both kinds of route):**
- **Spawns:** the full QuestieDB TBC NPC spawn list (`.cache/upstream/questiedb/data/TBC/tbcNpcDB.lua`, the commit recorded in `TBCGuide/Database/QuestieTBC/UPSTREAM_PROVENANCE.md`; `tools/build.ps1 fetch`). It includes elite rank and faction for every NPC.
- **Map sizes in yards:** the client's `UiMapAssignment` table (wago.tools, build 2.5.6.70577; now fetched with the other client tables).
- **Quest objectives and drop sources:** the bundled quest and item data. Kill targets come from the quest's kill list; drop sources from the item's NPC-drop list.

### The four hand-made routes

1. **Pick points:** each point is the centre of a cluster of real spawns. Nothing is a circle around a quest marker.
2. **Order:** the points are ordered by hand around obstacles, using nearby NPCs' known positions to place buildings and the Aldrassil trunk.
3. **Sample the legs:** every leg is sampled every 5 yards; each sample must lie within 50 yards of a known spawn of the route's targets.

| Route | Target spawns used | Worst leg gap | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kobold Vermin | 31 | 23 yd | One flat west-east camp north-west of Northshire Abbey. |
| Tough Wolf Meat | 55 | 48 yd | Loops around the abbey on open ground, at least 49 yd from the abbey's NPCs; wolves south of Northshire left out. |
| Young Nightsabers | 25 | 44 yd | East of Aldrassil; at least 64 yd from the NPC at the trunk's foot. |
| Young Thistle Boars | 24 | 37 yd | Between the trunk and the hostile Grellkin camp: at least 56 yd from the trunk's NPCs and 47 yd from the nearest Grellkin; goes around, never across. |

A single loop through both Balance of Nature targets was rejected: it had to cross the trunk or leave the glen.

### The researched routes (`tools/research/roam_propose.py`)

The tool applies the same standard to every candidate objective. It makes no decision a person has to make; it proposes, measures and records.

**Steps for each candidate objective:**
1. **Targets:** the quest's kill target, or the item's non-elite NPC droppers that spawn in the guide's zone.
2. **Spawn area:** spawns closer than 45 yards are joined into areas. The area chosen is the largest one, weighted towards the guide's previous step (where you arrive from).
3. **Too small:** fewer than 8 spawns or under 70 yards across means a static waypoint is preferred.
4. **Points:** 3 to 8 cluster centres, at 20-yard or 15-yard radius. Every pair is at least the two radii + 10 yards apart.
5. **The loop:** it must never cross itself, and no leg may run through another point's circle. Every leg is sampled every 5 yards; each sample must stay:
   - within 50 yards of a target spawn (30 in strict zones);
   - at least 25 yards from any elite or rare non-target spawn;
   - at least 60 yards from NPCs friendly only to the other faction (towns and guards).

   The shortest loop that passes all of these is used.
6. **Not written yet:** objectives whose text or guide step mentions a cave, mine, crypt, burrow, lair, den, tunnel and the like are blocked: spawn data has no height. So are objectives with elite targets.

**Human review** (`docs/research/roaming-overrides.csv`): no route is written until its zone has been reviewed.

| Batch | Zones | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alliance starting zones | normal |
| 2 | Horde starting zones | normal |
| 3 | Alliance 10-30 | normal |
| 4 | Horde 10-30 | normal |
| 5 | 30-50 | normal; strict in Badlands, Thousand Needles, Feralas, The Hinterlands |
| 6 | 50-60 | normal; strict in Tanaris, Searing Gorge, Un'Goro Crater, Winterspring, Burning Steppes |
| 7 | Outland | normal; strict in Blade's Edge, Netherstorm, Shadowmoon Valley |

- **Strict** means the 30-yard leg limit, for zones with cliffs, mesas, lava, void edges or multi-level coves.
- **Per quest:** after reading every proposal with the guide's own step text, 11 quests were blocked by hand:
  - **Underground:** Vile Familiars, Gold Dust Exchange, Night Web's Hollow, Red Leather Bandanas (Jangolode Mine).
  - **Multi-level:** Firewing Point (two quests), the Gol'Bolar quarry.
  - **Void edge:** Void Ridge.
  - **Guide text and quest data disagree on the source:** Raene's Cleansing, Dusky Crab Cakes, Bashal'Aran.

**What "verified" means for these routes:**
- **Data/schema:** the compiler checks the quest, objective, targets, drop sources, radius, spacing and coarse spawn proximity on every build (`tools/src/RoamRoutes.cs`). `tests/test_roam.lua` checks every compiled route's map, points, loop and named objectives, one route per objective, and that each route's quest has a guide step.
- **Source-verified spawn coverage:** every point is a centre of real spawns; every leg was sampled against the full spawn list.
- **Reviewed path plausibility:** the zone-level and per-quest review above.
- **Not in the game:** spawn data gives positions, not terrain or height. Straight legs inside a spawn area run over ground the mobs walk on, but a small obstacle on a line can't be ruled out. None of these routes has been walked in the game client.

**Runtime safeguard added for this expansion:** each route objective carries the English name of its target or item. On English clients a route runs only if the quest-log objective text names it. That catches a quest whose log order differs from the data; other clients rely on order and type.

## Limitations and next steps

- **Not walked in game:** none of the routes has been walked in the game client. The in-game checklist (docs/COMPATIBILITY.md, "Roaming hunting routes") covers a sample across zones and factions.
- **Caves, mines and multi-level areas:** no routes yet (57 objectives). Spawn data has no height, so cave levels can't be told apart.
- **Next batch: "no loop fits" in normal zones** (82 objectives; the list is `docs/research/roaming-dispositions.csv` filtered on that reason). Biggest groups: Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Wetlands and The Barrens (6 each), Arathi Highlands and Tirisfal Glades (5 each).
  - Many are strips along a road or shore. A back-and-forth route (out along the strip and back) isn't supported by the loop-only format; adding it would be the change that unlocks most of them.
  - Others really are scattered, and the static waypoint stays.
- **No drop source in the guide's zone** (54 objectives): some items drop from NPCs in a neighbouring zone, or from sub-zone ids the spawn data files elsewhere. These need a per-quest look.
- **Guide text disagrees with the quest data** (3 quests): check in game which mobs drop the item, then fix the guide text or the data.
- **Map display:** route points aren't drawn on the world map; only the current point is.
- **Language:** new chat and step-window text is English, like the rest of TBCGuide's own text. The objective text in the label comes from the client.
