# 1.0 route pass (levels 1-30)

The 448 quests added in 0.6.0 were placed by a script. This pass reorders those guides, checks every pickup's level gate against an XP model, adds notes, and keeps saved progress pointing at the right steps. Nothing here has been played in the game client.

## What was changed, and how it was found

**Which steps are from 0.6.0 (confirmed, from git):**
- Commit `700bd50` (0.6.0) added exactly 1401 lines to 25 guide files and deleted none (`git diff 536d86c 700bd50 -- guides-src`).
- Those 25 files are the scope of this pass. The other guides are unchanged.

**Reordering** (repeatable, deterministic tool; reviewed through the route audit, not by hand line by line):
- **Segments:** each guide is split at the steps that must stay where they are. These are travel transitions (fly, hearthstone, travel, guide links), `level`/`grind` checkpoints, `bind` and `train` steps.
- **Units:** inside a segment, every quest step (with the race/class variant lines of the same pickup, objective or turn-in) is one unit.
  - A `goto` stays with the step after it.
  - Text-only steps stay with the step before them.
- **Optimisation:** a dependency sort followed by a local search (moving one unit or a short block) lowers travel. It never breaks:
  - accept before objective before turn-in;
  - every turn-in line of a prerequisite before the first pickup line of the quest it unlocks;
  - level gates: a pickup never moves to a point where the XP model says you are too low, if it wasn't already gated there.

  Moves that don't fit these rules are rejected.
- **Validation:** the route audit (`tools/build.ps1 routes`) then walks all 52 race/class routes. It reports 0 order errors and 0 quests left in the log, with a peak quest log of 22/25.

**What reordering can't know:** terrain, roads, caves, flight times or swimming. Travel is measured as straight lines on the zone map, plus a fixed cost per zone change. Treat the travel figures as an approximation; they only say whether the route got shorter in the same units.

## Measuring travel

**Per-character travel** (`docs/ROUTE_AUDIT.md`, column *Per-character travel*):
- For each of the 52 race/class routes, sum the straight-line distances in zone-map percent between consecutive located steps that route sees, plus 25 for each zone change. Average that over the routes that use the guide.
- Class quests for other classes don't count. That's why this column, not the all-steps *Distance*, is the fair comparison.
- **Same method, three versions:** all three were measured with the same tool on the same database: 0.5.0 (before the import), 0.7.0 (the scripted placement) and now.

**Totals over the 25 guides:** 8420 in 0.5.0 → 15646 in 0.7.0 → **9987 now**. That is 36% less than 0.7.0, with every added quest kept.

## Level gates and the XP model

**Model inputs:**
- **Quest XP:** QuestieDB's TBC quest XP table (`support/QuestXP/xpDB-tbc.lua`, the TBC value per quest). It's reduced to 80/60/40/20/10% when you are 6/7/8/9/10+ levels above the quest (warcraft.wiki.gg, *Experience point*).
- **XP to level:** the post-2.3 table (warcraft.wiki.gg, *Experience to level*, "pre-Wrath"), which is the TBC Classic ruleset.

**Assumptions (not data):**
- Kills add half the quest XP earned, so kills make up a third of all XP. Real kill XP depends on how you play.
- Rested XP, discovery XP, groups and dungeons are **not** counted, so the model is cautious.
- Class and race steps count only on routes that see them.
- `level`/`grind` steps lift the model to their level, as if you grind there.

**Pickups flagged as needing a higher level than the model has reached:** 206 in 0.7.0 → **116 now** (on these guides).

**What happens if you arrive too low:** the remaining gated pickups have a note giving the level needed. The engine now **skips a pickup you are too low for**, with the reason shown, instead of blocking the guide.
- **You level before moving on:** the pickup comes back.
- **You have already moved on:** it stays skipped, so the guide never sends you back. Its objective and turn-in steps are skipped with it.

## Notes

Notes are added only from the bundled QuestieDB data:
- which mobs drop a quest item;
- which object to click;
- which kill targets are elite;
- escort quests;
- the zone where a turn-in happens, when the finisher stands in exactly one other zone;
- the level needed on gated pickups.

There is no copied prose and no invented tactics. Steps that already had a note were left alone. Notes added: **514**.

## Saved progress

- **Step map:** guides whose step order changed have a layout number and an old-to-new step map (`TBCGuide/Guides/Layouts.lua`, generated by matching each 0.7.0 step to its new position: 25 guides, every step matched).
- **Applied once:** the first time a character opens such a guide, its done, skipped and current step indices are moved to the new positions.
- **Characters from before 0.6.0:** their progress was already shifted by 0.6.0 and can't be mapped. Quest steps re-check themselves against your quest log, so only manual steps (notes, travel) may need a click.

## Remaining uncertainties

- **Barrens and Duskwood still travel more than in 0.5.0.** They now hold twice as many quests, and part of the extra travel is class-quest trips to Thunder Bluff, Orgrimmar and Stormwind. Those trips are tied to class chains that can't move without breaking them.
- **Pickups still 3+ levels short:** some remain where the model says you'll be 3 or more levels short (9 in the Barrens, 10 in Redridge, 4 in Dun Morogh).
  - These are mostly higher-level quests the 0.6.0 import put in an earlier zone.
  - The engine skips them for under-level players, so they never block.
  - Moving them to a later guide is a possible follow-up.
- **Darkshore gated pickups rose** from 7 to 10. Reordering its single long segment moved some pickups earlier, ahead of the turn-ins that give the XP for them. They're 1-3 levels short and are skipped if you're too low.
- **The XP model is an estimate.** A player who kills more, uses rested XP or groups will be higher, and the gates matter less. Someone who skips optional quests will be lower, and the engine's level skip covers that.
- **Notes are only as good as the bundled data.** For example, a drop source missing from QuestieDB means no drop note.
- **Not checked by the tool:** caves, terrain and timed or escort events beyond the data's escort flag. Escorts are marked, but their timing isn't modelled.
- **Not played.** A playthrough of the four priority zones is the most useful in-game check (see the checklist in the release notes).

Reproducing: the reorder, note and step-map tools were run once for this pass. The measurements come from `tools/build.ps1 routes` on each version's `guides-src`.
