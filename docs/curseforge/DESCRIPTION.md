# TBCGuide

**Free** leveling guides from 1 to 70 for **The Burning Crusade Classic** (Anniversary realms, 2.5.6), with a built-in quest database, a talent guide and quest automation. There are no guide packs, accounts or subscriptions, and you don't need to install Questie.

## Features

- **Level 1-70 guides for every race and class**: 54 guides covering all 10 starting zones (including Draenei and Blood Elf), 32 Azeroth zones and all 7 Outland leveling zones. Every one of the 52 race/class routes is checked step by step: no quest is picked up before the NPC offers it, the quest log never overflows, and nothing is picked up that is never turned in.
- **Built-in TBC quest database** (derived from QuestieDB) for quest names, objectives, prerequisites, givers and coordinates.
- **Automatic progress** from your quest log, completed quests, level, hearthstone and flight paths. Every step can also be completed or skipped by hand.
- **Navigation**: an arrow, a world-map pin and optional TomTom waypoints.
- **Quest automation** for the quests in your guide:
  - accepts and turns in your active guide's quests at quest NPCs;
  - **never accepts escort quests**: it tells you to take them yourself when you're ready;
  - **never picks your reward**: when there's a choice, the window waits for you;
  - hold **Shift** to skip it for one conversation.
- **Junk selling** (off by default): sells grey items at vendors. Quest items, items your quests need, and items or categories you protect are always kept.
- **Orphaned quests**: a button on the quest log lists quests your route will never turn in, and abandons the ones you pick after you confirm.
- **Talent guide and calculator** for all nine classes: 21 builds for leveling, tanking, healing and DPS, checked against the 2.5.6 client data.
- **Class leveling tips** for every class.

## Commands

`/tbcg` shows or hides the guide, `/tbcg guide` opens the guide browser, `/tbcg talents` opens the talent guide, `/tbcg automation` opens the automation settings, `/tbcg orphans` opens the orphaned-quest list, and `/tbcg help` lists everything.

## Status

The guides and core features have been played in the game. The 0.2.0 additions (quest automation, junk selling, orphaned quests and the route fixes) are released as **beta** until they have been tested in the client. Please report problems on GitHub.
